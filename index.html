Questions

What does the venue pay?

Nothing. Not for the board, the printing, the delivery or the pens. The six advertisers cover all of it.

Who owns the board?

We do, and we look after it. If it gets damaged or looks tired we replace it. The venue just puts it out and brings it in.

Can the venue reject an advertiser?

Yes. We show the venue every business before anything is printed, and they can veto a competitor or anyone else they are not comfortable with.

What happens after twelve months?

Advertisers choose whether to renew. We reprint the ad panels with the new lineup and the board stays where it is.

Can I change my ad mid year?

Get in touch. A small change like a new phone number can usually wait for the next reprint. A full redesign means a new panel and a print cost.

What if the venue closes or moves?

We move the board to another venue nearby and reprint the header. Your spot keeps running for the rest of the twelve months.

Where do you operate?

Perth metro, one area at a time, so every board carries genuinely local advertisers rather than businesses from the other side of the city.
